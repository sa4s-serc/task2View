Judge only these four things, and check claims against the code wherever you are not sure:
1. For each concern detail: does the view answer it for this stakeholder (yes, partly, no)? Say what is missing.
2. Is the level of detail right for this stakeholder and perspective (too detailed, right, too abstract)? If the

detail unanswered, or is too detailed for the stakeholder, needs revision.
